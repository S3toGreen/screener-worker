ODF MARKET DATA

Daily TWSE + TPEx session data. Public, CORS-open, edge-cached.

EndpointPayload
/api/snapshot Every listed stock and fund (ETF/ETN/REIT) for the latest session: 52 positional columns (price, turnover, valuation, foreign holding, institutional flows, margin, risk flags and the rolling 5/20-session figures).
/api/history/{symbol} One symbol's rolling history, up to 260 sessions: OHLC, volume, turnover and institutional flows, index-aligned on dates.
Updated once per session after the close. Both endpoints are cached at the edge for up to an hour.
